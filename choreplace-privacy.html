Chore Place Privacy Statement

Last updated: June 15, 2026

Overview

Chore Place is a household chore management app. It stores household, profile, and task information locally on the device and syncs eligible data through Apple CloudKit using the app's iCloud container.

What Data the App Stores

Chore Place may store:

  • Household names
  • Family member profile names and roles
  • Task titles, details, due dates, reminders, recurrence settings, and completion state
  • Device-to-profile claim state used to connect a device to a family member
  • Notification preferences stored on-device
  • Optional photo proof images attached to completed chores

How Data Is Stored and Synced

  • Local app data is stored on-device using SwiftData.
  • Synced household data is stored in Apple CloudKit for the app's container: iCloud.com.seanobrien.Chore-Place.
  • Chore Place uses CloudKit private and shared databases for syncing and household sharing.
  • Chore Place does not use a separate custom backend server for household, profile, or task data.

Household Sharing

  • Household data is shared only with people who join the same household invite.
  • People outside a household share should not be able to view that household's data inside the app.
  • The app's sharing model depends on Apple CloudKit sharing.

Photo Proof

  • Photo proof is intended only for images of completed chores.
  • Users should not photograph people, faces, or private documents.
  • Photo proof images are temporary and are deleted after adult review, within 72 hours of completion, or sooner if they are no longer needed.

Notifications

Chore Place may schedule local notifications on a user's device for reminders, overdue chores, and missed chores, depending on that device's settings and the profile linked to the device.

No Third-Party Analytics or Custom Chore Backend

As of this statement, Chore Place does not use a separate third-party analytics service or a custom chore-data server to store household task data.

Important CloudKit Container Note

Although Chore Place does not expose household data to the developer through the normal in-app experience, synced data is stored in the app's Apple CloudKit container. Administrative access to Apple developer tooling is separate from normal user-facing app behavior.

Contact and Updates

If this privacy behavior changes, this statement should be updated before broader distribution.
