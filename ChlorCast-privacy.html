Privacy Policy – ChlorCast

Effective Date: June 2026

ChlorCast is a pool chemistry and chlorine forecasting application.

Information Collection

ChlorCast does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information.

Pool chemistry readings, forecasts, and application settings may be stored locally on the user’s device.

ChlorCast may access internet-based weather forecast information to help estimate chlorine loss and pool conditions.

No personally identifiable information is transmitted or shared by the app.

Third-Party Services

ChlorCast does not use third-party advertising or analytics services.

Data Security

Information stored by the app remains on the user’s device.
