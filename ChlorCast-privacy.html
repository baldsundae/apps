Privacy Policy – ChlorCast

Effective Date: June 2026

ChlorCast is a pool chemistry and chlorine forecasting application.

Information Collection

ChlorCast does not collect personal information from users.

Pool chemistry readings and app settings may be stored locally on the user’s device.

Weather Data

The app may use weather forecast information to estimate chlorine loss and pool conditions.

Third-Party Services

ChlorCast may rely on external weather data providers to deliver forecast information.

Data Security

Any locally stored information remains on the user’s device.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated periodically.
