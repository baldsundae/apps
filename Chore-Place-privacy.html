Privacy Policy – Chore Place

Effective Date: June 2026

Chore Place is a household and chore management application designed for families and shared households.

Information Collection and Use

Chore Place stores household, chore, and user-generated app content using Apple iCloud and CloudKit services associated with the user’s Apple ID.

The app does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.

Cloud Synchronization

If enabled by the user, data may be synchronized through Apple iCloud services to allow shared household functionality across authorized devices and users.

Third-Party Services

Chore Place relies on Apple services including iCloud and CloudKit for synchronization and data storage.

Data Security

Data security is managed through Apple’s iCloud infrastructure and security systems.

Children’s Privacy

The app is not directed toward children under 13 without parental supervision.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated periodically.
